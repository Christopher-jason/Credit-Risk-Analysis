CREDIT RISK MODEL: BUSINESS SUMMARY
Probability of Default (PD) Model — Loan Portfolio


1. PURPOSE

This model estimates the Probability of Default (PD) for loan applicants,
using historical loan data to identify which applicant and loan
characteristics are associated with higher default risk.


2. THE DATA

- 58,645 historical loan applications, no missing data
- Overall default rate: 14.24%

[insert fig: loan_status_distribution.png]


3. WHAT DRIVES DEFAULT RISK

Loan grade is the strongest predictor of default:
  - Grade A: ~4.9%  |  Grade G: ~81.8%

Home ownership is the second-strongest signal:
  - Renters: ~22.3%  |  Mortgage: ~6.0%  |  Own outright: ~1.4%

6. RECOMMENDATION

The scorecard model is recommended for any decision that must be
explained to an applicant or regulator. Tree-based models may offer a
modest accuracy edge but sacrifice transparency.


7. LIMITATIONS

- Trained on a single historical snapshot; would need retraining if
  the applicant population shifts over time (see PSI above)
- Two erroneous data entries (age/employment length = 123) were
  identified and corrected via median imputation
- Not yet tested for fairness/bias across protected characteristics
